The Enchanted Forest

Chapter I: The Cartographer's Compass

by Elena Brightwood

The morning mist clung to the ancient silver pines like spider silk, dampening the sound of Clara’s footsteps as she crossed the uncharted boundary of the Whispering Woods. In her leather satchel lay three centuries of kingdom maps, every one of them leaving this exact valley blank, washed in pale parchment ink with the single annotation: Terra Ignota.

She lifted her brass compass. Instead of spinning toward the magnetic north, the needle trembled, pointed south toward a towering arch of emerald moss, and then began to turn in slow, deliberate circles—as if keeping time with a heartbeat buried deep under the roots.

"You shouldn't be drawing what wants to remain hidden," a voice drifted from the canopy above. Clara reached instinctively for her surveyor’s knife, but all that met her gaze was an iridescent moth the size of a falcon, resting upon a glowing silver branch that hadn't been there a second before.

The forest was not a landscape; it was an architecture of living memory. And with every step she took toward the heartwood grove, the path behind her rewrote itself, sealing the exit with roots woven as tight as iron gates.
